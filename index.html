About me

I'm a fairly typical software engineer most of my day is spent moving between an IDE, documentation, and a browser tab full of Stack Overflow and GitHub issues. I like the problem-solving side of the job more than any one language or framework: figuring out why something breaks, then figuring out the cleanest way to stop it breaking again.

Working IT alongside development has shaped how I build. Once you've been the person woken up because a server or a mail server went down, you start writing software differently — thinking about what happens when things fail, not just when they work. I'm still learning constantly and still enjoy the process more than I let on when a bug has been open for six hours.

Currently I'm a Junior Software Developer at Doctor on Call, where I work on healthcare technology that connects patients and providers.
